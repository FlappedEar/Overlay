FlappedEar Overlays User Guide

FlappedEar Overlays (formerly FlappedEar Telemetry) is a macOS application for motorsport video and data. It puts live telemetry overlays on your onboard video, for one session or for a whole track day of recordings.

About the pictures: every screenshot in this guide is taken from the application's own controller and interface with real data: the onboard GoPro video and RaceChrono Pro VBO of Session 5 at Jastrząb on 29 August 2026, synchronized by Auto Sync, and all six sessions of that day in one event. How the pictures are produced is described in the capture tools.

What it does

Overlay editor

  • Load an MP4 or MOV video and a RaceChrono VBO or RCZ recording.
  • Synchronize them automatically from GoPro GPS, or by hand.
  • Arrange speed, RPM, pedals, G-force, track map, heart rate, tyre, lap time and delta widgets, or start from a template.
  • Export an H.265/HEVC MP4 of the whole video, a timecode range, or a single hot lap.

Track days

  • Open a track day saved by FlappedEar Telemetry, with each recording as a run, and pick the run to edit in the header.
  • Export finds the day's best lap across all runs, from lap detection, and offers it as the hot lap.

Terms used in this guide

TermMeaning
TelemetryData recorded by a lap timer or data logger: GPS, speed, RPM, pedals, G, temperatures, heart rate.
ChannelOne recorded quantity in a telemetry file, for example velocity or rpm-obd.
WidgetOne overlay element drawn over the video.
TemplateA saved arrangement of widgets.
Event, run, lapA track day, one session on track, and one timed lap of a session.
Timing gateThe start/finish line stored in the telemetry file, used to cut laps.
Eligible lapA complete lap with good GPS on the day's layout, used to find the day's best lap.
