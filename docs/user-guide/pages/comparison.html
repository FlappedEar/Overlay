Comparing two laps

Lap comparison places lap A and lap B on one shared track position axis, so you can see where on track one lap gained or lost time and what the channels did differently.

Choosing laps A and B

  1. In Day results click Compare laps…. The dialog Compare laps · A / B opens.
  2. Pick Lap A (green) and Lap B (orange). Each slot loads its recording independently and shows Ready when done. Inspect opens a slot's lap on its own; Clear empties it.
  3. Click Compare A / B.

Shortcuts in the dialog: Swap A / B, Best of A's run → B and Best of group → B.

Only eligible complete laps can be chosen, and once one slot is filled the other offers laps from the same comparison group only. Laps may come from different runs of the day. The chosen pair, zoom and channels are saved with the project.

The shared track position axis

The horizontal axis is distance in metres along lap A's GPS line, starting at the timing gate. Lap B is placed on the same axis by its position on track, not by its own travelled distance. A corner is therefore at the same place for both laps, even if they took different lines.

The comparison view

The header shows both laps (run, lap number, time) and their status. If the two laps belong to incompatible track configurations, a warning says so. ← All laps or Esc returns to the list.

Overlay charts

  • Up to four channels, chosen as in single-lap view. Only channels that both laps recorded are offered, plus Δ time.
  • Defaults: Δ time, speed, throttle and brake.
  • Each channel shows lap A in green and lap B in orange. Hover shows the A value, the B value and their difference (A − B).
  • Δ time (A−B) is one yellow line: the running time difference. Positive means A is behind, negative means A is ahead.
  • Zoom: scroll to zoom around the pointer, scroll sideways to pan, drag to select a range, double-click or Reset zoom to return. Charts, map, G-G and video all share one zoom range and one cursor.

Overlay map

Both GPS traces on one scale, with markers showing where each car was at the cursor position. The zoomed range is highlighted.

The layer selector colours one lap's line by a value. Use the A and B buttons to choose which lap is coloured; the other stays visible, dimmed.

Layers that a lap did not record are marked not recorded. The brake layer only shows a recorded brake channel; it is never estimated from deceleration. Gaps end the line.

Side columns

Three buttons open one side column at a time:

  • Corner Analyzer: segment-by-segment metrics. See Corner Analyzer.
  • G-G: see below.
  • Video: see below.

G-G diagram

A scatter of lateral against longitudinal G for both laps over the current zoom range (Whole lap or Selected stretch). Accelerating is up, braking down, with rings every 0.5 g. The table lists Peak lateral, Peak braking, Peak combined and the number of Samples for A and B.

These are observed accelerations, not a share of available grip. Values beyond ±4 g are treated as faulty and left out; peaks use every remaining sample.

Side-by-side video

When both runs have synchronized video, the Video column shows lap A and lap B at the same point on track. Press play: one lap plays in real time and the other keeps its place on track, so it runs ahead or behind where the laps differ. Charts and map follow. If a run has no video, its video is missing or changed, or its footage does not cover the point, a message says so.
